Waitlist privacy notice

This notice covers the typein early-access waitlist.

What we collect

We collect the business type and WhatsApp number you submit, along with the time of your request and your agreement to receive early-access updates.

How we use it

We use this information to manage the early-access list and contact you on WhatsApp about typein. Joining the list does not create a booking or payment.

Storage and access

Waitlist entries are stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Neon. The site and API hosting providers process requests needed to run the waitlist. Access to entries is limited to people operating typein.

Your choices

To ask about your entry or request its deletion, email imvinayachandran@gmail.com. You can also tell us to stop contacting you when we message you.
